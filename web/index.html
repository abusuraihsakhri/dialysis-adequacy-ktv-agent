Scope and limitations

Results are approximations for hemodialysis and depend on correct post-treatment blood sampling, access type and treatment conditions. The eKt/V rate equation assumes arterial access and at least 2 hours of dialysis. Weekly stdKt/V uses a fixed-volume model and excludes residual kidney function; nPCR assumes three sessions per week and negligible residual renal urea clearance.

For research and education, not clinical decision-making or automated prescribing. Do not enter patient identifiers. Values are processed locally; the site contains no telemetry or external scripts.

References: KDOQI 2015; Leypoldt et al. (2003); Depner & Daugirdas (1996).
